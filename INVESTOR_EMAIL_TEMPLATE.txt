## MONTHLY TOP-UP SYSTEM (The "ETF" Part)

This isn't a traditional managed account. It's ETF-style: transparent, owner-verified, and disciplined.

### How It Works
Every month, you add capital. The EA automatically scales position sizes.

Example:
  Month 1: Deposit $1,000 → EA trades at 0.1 lot size
  Month 2: You earn +$200, deposit +$500 → Account = $1,700 → EA scales to 0.17 lots
  Month 3: You earn +$100, deposit +$500 → Account = $2,300 → EA scales to 0.23 lots

Result: Compounding growth through consistent monthly additions.
